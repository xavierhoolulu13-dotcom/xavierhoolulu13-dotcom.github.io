    You're Not on the List

    Watch somebody look for lunch. Phone out. Two words. "Poke near me." Three names come up. Maybe four. That's the whole list. You're not on it. Nothing wrong with your food. Nothing wrong with your door. You just never claimed the little map card with your name on it. Hours nobody updated. No photos.
