In a shop, you call the order out. Everybody hears it. Cook, counter, whoever's bagging. Nobody has to be told twice.

Software doesn't usually work like that. You do your part, then you hand it to the next person. And something gets lost in the handoff. It always does.

That's the thing I built Hoolulu Hub to kill.

One board. Every worker sees the whole thing — not just their slice. The invoice. The reminder. The follow-up. All of it, on the same board, in front of everybody.

Nothing gets dropped between two people. Because there's no between.

**Tell me the last thing that got lost in a handoff at your shop.**

Two questions:

1. Where in your day does something slip between you and somebody else?

2. Would it help if everybody saw the whole board, not just their part?

— Xavier
