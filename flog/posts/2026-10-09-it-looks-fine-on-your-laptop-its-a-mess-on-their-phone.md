# It Looks Fine on Your Laptop. It's a Mess on Their Phone.

You built your website at the kitchen table. On the laptop. It looked good.

But that's not where they're looking. They're on the sidewalk. On a phone. One hand. Sun in their eyes.

And on that little screen, your site is a mess. The words run off the side. The buttons are too small to tap. The menu won't open.

So they pinch and squint for a second. Then they're gone.

Here's the test. Open your own site on your phone. Right now. Try to find your phone number. If it takes more than a few seconds, that's what your customers get too.

**Open your site on your phone. Try to call yourself. Tell me how long it took.**

Two questions:

1. When's the last time you looked at your own site on a phone?
2. Could you find your own phone number in five seconds?

— Xavier
