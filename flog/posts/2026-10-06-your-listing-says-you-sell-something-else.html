Your Listing Says You Sell Something Else

October 6, 2026 · Honolulu local business visibility

Somebody wants what you sell. They type it in. You don't come up.

Not because you're missing. You're on the map. You've got reviews. Your pin's in the right spot.

It's just filed under the wrong thing. The card says you're a caterer. You run a lunch counter. Or it says you're a beauty salon, and you're a barber.

Somebody chose that category years ago, back when the listing got set up. Nobody's looked at it since.

So the search that should hand them you hands them somebody else.

Open your listing. Find the category. Read it out loud. Is that what you sell?

Two questions:

  1. What do you actually sell — and does the listing say it?
  2. If you read your own category out loud, would a customer recognize you?

— Xavier
