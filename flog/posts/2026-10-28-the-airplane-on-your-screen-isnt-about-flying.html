The Airplane on Your Screen Isn't About Flying

October 28, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

My mother called me from a restaurant. "My phone says No Service."

I asked if she was holding it. She was.

Then I asked the question that fixes it half the time. Is there a little airplane at the top of your screen?

Silence. "There is."

The airplane is airplane mode. It cuts the phone off from the world on purpose. You turn it on for a flight. Then you forget, and it stays on for a week.

Here is the check. Look at the very top of your screen. A small airplane up there means airplane mode is on.

Swipe down from the top of the screen. On an iPhone, swipe from the top right corner. Find the airplane and tap it once. The bars come back.

Look at the top of your screen right now. Is there an airplane up there?

Two questions.

  1. Has "No Service" ever cost you a call you were waiting on?
  2. When did you last look at the top of your phone, instead of only the middle?
