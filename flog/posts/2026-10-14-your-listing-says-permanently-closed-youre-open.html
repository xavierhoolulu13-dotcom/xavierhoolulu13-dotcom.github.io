Your Listing Says Permanently Closed. You're Open.

October 14, 2026 · Honolulu local business visibility

Somebody looks you up. Your name comes up. And under it, in plain words: Permanently closed.

You're not. You're right here. Same door, same hours.

But they don't know that. They see closed, they believe it, and they move on to the next place.

It happens by accident. A temporary closure gets marked wrong. Somebody taps the wrong button. And it just sits there.

You'd never know. Nobody calls to tell you your shop is dead online.

Look up your own shop. Read what it says under your name. Does it say closed?

Two questions:

  1. When did you last look at your own listing the way a customer does?
  2. If it said you were closed, how long would it take you to find out?

— Xavier
