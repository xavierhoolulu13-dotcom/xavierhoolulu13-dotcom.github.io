The best employee you ever had knew everything.

Which form. Which supplier. Which number to call when the freezer starts humming wrong. None of it written down. All of it in one head.

Then they left.

For a month, everybody guesses. The new person asks a question nobody can answer. In November you find out something didn't get filed in March.

Hoolulu Hub writes the steps down as the work happens. Not in a binder nobody opens. As something that runs. The steps stay after the person doesn't.

**Pick one job only you know how to do. Write it down this week — one step at a time, like you're telling a new hire.**

Two questions.

1. What's the one job in your shop that only one person knows?
2. If that person called in sick for a month, what breaks first?
