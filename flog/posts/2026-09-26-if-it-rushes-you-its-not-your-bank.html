If It Rushes You, It's Not Your Bank

September 26, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

Somebody reads you a text.

"Your account is locked. Tap here to verify."

Is it real? Here's the test I hand out. It works almost every time.

Read it out loud. Slowly.

If it sounds like it's rushing you, it's not your bank. Your bank already has your number. Nobody who already has your money needs to ask for it again by text.

Read it out loud. If it makes you feel panicked, that's the whole trick. The panic is the product.

Delete it. Then call the number on the back of your card. Not the number in the text.

Send this to one person who'd tap first and ask later.

Two questions:

  1. What's the last message that made you feel rushed?
  2. Who do you know who needs to read this out loud?

— Xavier
