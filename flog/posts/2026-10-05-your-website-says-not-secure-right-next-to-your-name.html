Your Website Says Not Secure. Right Next to Your Name.

October 5, 2026 · XKSH808 Digital Services Hawaii

Somebody looks you up. Your name comes up. And right there in the address bar, in front of your shop's name, it says: Not Secure.

They don't know what that means. They just know it sounds bad. So they back out and call the next place.

Here's what it is. Your site is missing its little certificate — the thing that makes the browser trust it. It's not a hack. Nothing's broken. It just expired, or it was never set up.

It's a small fix. But until it's done, every customer sees that warning before they see you.

Open your website on your phone. Look at the address bar. Tell me what it says.

Two questions:

  1. Have you ever looked at your own site on your phone?
  2. What does the address bar say next to your name?
