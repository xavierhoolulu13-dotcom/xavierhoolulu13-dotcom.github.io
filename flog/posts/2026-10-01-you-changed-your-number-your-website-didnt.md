# You Changed Your Number. Your Website Didn't.

Somebody's phone breaks. Water, drop, whatever.

So you get a new number. You tell your friends. You tell your regulars. You tell the guy who does your insurance.

You forget the website.

That number on your contact page? Still the old one. Somebody else has it now. Maybe a stranger. Maybe nobody.

And here's the thing. It doesn't look wrong. A number is just a number. Nobody calls it to test it.

So a customer calls. It rings in somebody's kitchen. They hang up. Then they call the next shop on the list.

**Send me your website. I'll read you the number you've got on there, out loud, right now.**

Two questions:

1. When did you last change your phone number — and who did you remember to tell?
2. Do you actually know what number's on your own site today?

— Xavier
