The Map Sends Them to the Wrong Block

October 2, 2026 · Honolulu local business visibility

Somebody's looking for you. They pull up the map and hit directions.

It takes them two blocks over. A parking lot. Somebody's driveway.

They look around. No shop. They give up and go somewhere else.

You never hear about it. Nobody calls to say your pin's in the wrong place. They just don't come.

The pin got dropped once, when the listing was made. Probably by somebody who's never stood on your corner. And it's been sitting there ever since.

Pull up your own shop on the map. Hit directions. See where it takes you.

Two questions:

  1. Have you ever actually gotten directions to your own shop?
  2. If a customer did it today, where would they end up?

— Xavier
