The Words Are Small. You Can Make Them Bigger.

October 8, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

You're holding the phone out at arm's length. Trying to read the menu. It's not working.

Your eyes aren't the problem. The words are just small.

You can fix that. Open Settings. Look for Display. In there, find Text Size. Slide it toward the big end. The words on the screen get bigger as you slide.

That's it. It stays that way. Every screen, every app.

Hand me your phone and I'll set it up with you — we'll find the size that works.

Two questions:

  1. Where do you usually hold your phone when you're reading it?
  2. What's the one thing you're always squinting at?
