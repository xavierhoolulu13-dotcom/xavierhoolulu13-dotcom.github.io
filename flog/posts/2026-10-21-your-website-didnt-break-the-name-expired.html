Your Website Didn't Break. The Name Expired.

October 21, 2026 · XKSH808 Digital Services Hawaii

The website didn't break. The name it lives at expired.

You go to send somebody the link. You type it in yourself first, just to check.

And it's not your shop. It's a page of ads. “Related Searches.” A button offering to sell you the name back.

Nothing got hacked. Nothing got deleted. The name just ran out. Like a car registration you forgot about, except nobody mails you the warning you'd actually notice.

Here's what most people don't know. The name isn't part of the website. Two bills. Two logins. You keep paying one and forget the other, and the site you're still paying for goes dark with it.

And a lapsed name usually doesn't sit empty. Somebody picks it up and fills it with ads. Your customers land there thinking it's you.

Go find the email from whoever you bought the name from. Search your inbox for “renewal.” Then put a reminder a month before the date.

Two questions.

  1. Do you know the date your name comes due?
  2. Who has the login — you, or somebody who stopped answering the phone?

— Xavier
