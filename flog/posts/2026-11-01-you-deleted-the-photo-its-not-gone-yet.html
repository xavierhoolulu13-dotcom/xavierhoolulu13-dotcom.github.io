You Deleted the Photo. It's Not Gone Yet.

November 1, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

My aunt deleted a photo of her granddaughter. She sat down. She cried. She was sure it was gone.

It wasn't. The phone doesn't throw things away the first time you hit delete. It puts them in a folder and waits.

Open Photos. Scroll all the way down. Look for "Recently Deleted."

Your photo is in there. Tap it. Tap Recover. It goes back where it was.

It waits about a month. Then it's really gone.

Go look right now. Scroll to the bottom of your photos and find that folder. You might find something you thought you lost.

Two questions.

  1. What's the photo you'd most hate to lose?
  2. Did you find anything in there you forgot about?
