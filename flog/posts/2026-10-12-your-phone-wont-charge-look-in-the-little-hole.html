Your Phone Won't Charge. Look in the Little Hole.

October 12, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

You plug it in at night. Same cord, same spot. In the morning it's dead.

So you figure the battery's gone. Or the phone's done. Time for a new one.

Maybe not. Look at the little hole where the cord goes in.

Pocket lint. That's usually it. It packs down in there over the years, and the cord can't reach the metal anymore.

Turn the phone off. Get a wooden toothpick. Gently — gently — dig the fuzz out. Don't use anything metal.

Then plug it back in. See if it charges.

Before you buy a new phone, look in the hole. Tell me what you find.

Two questions:

  1. How long has your phone been "not charging right"?
  2. Did you know that little hole collects lint?
