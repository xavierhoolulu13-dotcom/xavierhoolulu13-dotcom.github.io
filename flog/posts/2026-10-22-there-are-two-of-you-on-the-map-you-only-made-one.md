# There Are Two of You on the Map. You Only Made One.

Someone types your shop name into their phone.

Two cards come up. Same name. Same street.

One says you close at five. The other says six.

One has your old phone number on it. The other has a photo from before you repainted.

You didn't make the second one. A customer made it. Or a directory. Or the marketing company that stopped calling you back. It just showed up.

Here's the part that stings. Your reviews are split across both. Nobody reads every review — they read the card that loads first. Sometimes that's the one with almost none on it.

Two pins means two ways to look closed, out of date, or like a place that can't be sure it exists.

**Search your shop name on your own phone. Count the cards. If there are two, the wrong one has to go — you claim it, then you close it.**

Two questions.

1. How many of you are on the map?

2. Does the duplicate have your good reviews, or a photo that isn't your shop anymore?

— Xavier
