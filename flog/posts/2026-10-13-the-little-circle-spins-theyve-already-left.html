The Little Circle Spins. They've Already Left.

October 13, 2026 · XKSH808 Digital Services Hawaii

You know the little circle. It spins while a page loads.

Somebody types your name into their phone. Taps your shop. The circle starts spinning.

One second. Two. They're standing on the sidewalk with somewhere to be.

So they hit back. And call the next place.

Your site isn't broken. It's just slow. And slow looks broken when you're the one waiting.

Open your own site on your phone. Count how long that circle spins. Then tell me the number.

Two questions:

  1. When you open your own site, does it come up right away?
  2. How long do you wait on a page before you give up?

— Xavier
