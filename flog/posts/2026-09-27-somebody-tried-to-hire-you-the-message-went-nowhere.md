# Somebody Tried to Hire You. The Message Went Nowhere.

A guy fills out the form on your website. Phone. Thumb. Hits send.

He sees "Thanks! We'll be in touch."

Then he waits. Nobody calls. So he moves on to the next shop.

You never saw it. The form has been quietly broken for months. Maybe the email address behind it is one you stopped checking in 2021.

Here's the thing about a contact form. When it breaks, it doesn't crash. It goes quiet. Nobody complains, because the person who tried already left.

Your website isn't a sign. It's a doorbell. And one that doesn't ring is worse than none — because people ring it, then walk away.

**Send me your website. I'll fill out your own contact form and tell you if it actually reaches you.**

Two questions:

1. If I emailed you through your own site right now, would you get it?
2. When did you last test that — not the site, the form?

— Xavier
