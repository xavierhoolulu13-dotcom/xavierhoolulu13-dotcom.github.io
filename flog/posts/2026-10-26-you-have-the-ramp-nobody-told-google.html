You Have the Ramp. Nobody Told Google.

October 26, 2026 · Honolulu local business visibility

There's a row of little labels under your shop's name on Google.

"Wheelchair accessible entrance." "Free parking." "Veteran-owned." "Good for kids."

Somebody pulls up your card and filters for a ramp. You disappear. Not because you don't have one. Because nobody ever told Google you do.

The ramp is real. The boxes are empty.

Same thing with parking. Half your customers drive, and nothing on your card says there's a lot out back. They circle the block twice and leave.

Open your listing on Google. Find the section called "Business information" or "Edit profile." Tick every box that's actually true about your shop. Untick the ones that aren't.

Two questions.

  1. What's one true thing about your shop that's nowhere on Google?
  2. Have you ever skipped a place because the listing made it look hard to get into?
