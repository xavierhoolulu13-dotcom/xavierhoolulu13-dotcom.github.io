# The Review You Never Answered Is Still Talking

Somebody leaves you a review.

Three stars. "Food was great. Waited a while."

You read it. You feel it. You close the app.

That review is still there. And now it's not just talking to you. It's talking to everybody deciding whether to walk in.

Here's what most owners miss. A reply isn't for the person who wrote it. They already ate and left. The reply is for the next ten people reading it.

Silence reads as nobody's home.

**Send me your last three reviews. I'll show you what a stranger sees when they read them.**

Two questions:

1. When did you last reply to a review — good or bad?
2. If a customer read your reviews right now, would they think someone's minding the shop?

— Xavier
