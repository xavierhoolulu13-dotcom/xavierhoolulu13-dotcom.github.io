You Closed at Six. It Didn't.

October 15, 2026 · Hoolulu Hub

You flip the sign. Lock the door. Drive home.

Dinner. Shower. Some TV.

Meanwhile the day's still not finished. Somebody left a review at 8. A reminder needs sending. The follow-up you meant to write.

A person clocks out. They should. The workspace doesn't have a shift to end.

It puts in its hours while you eat.

Next morning, open it up. See what got done while you were off. Tell me what you find.

Two questions:

  1. What's the thing that goes undone when you close?
  2. Would you rather it waited for you in the morning, or just got handled?

— Xavier
