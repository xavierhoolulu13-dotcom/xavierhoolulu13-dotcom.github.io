You Don't Learn It. You Just Tell It.

October 19, 2026 · Hoolulu Hub

Every piece of software I've ever bought wanted me to learn its words first.

Fields. Tabs. A dropdown with forty choices, and none of them mine.

I don't have the time. Neither do you.

So Hoolulu Hub goes the other way. You tell it the job in your words. "Remind the customer her order's in." "Post the special." "Ask the guy from Tuesday if he's coming back."

It does the job. Then it shows you what it did, so you can look.

No manual. No training. No forty choices.

Try it with one job. The one you keep forgetting.

Two questions.

  1. What's the one job you keep forgetting?
  2. If you could just say it out loud and have it done, what would you say?

— Xavier
