That's the part people picture wrong. They hear "automation" and see a machine out there doing things on its own. Fair. That would make me nervous too.

Mine drafts. It doesn't decide.

This morning there were three things waiting. A reply to a review. A note to a customer. A change to my hours. All written. All sitting there.

I read each one. Two went out. One I rewrote, because the draft was polite and I wasn't feeling polite.

The work gets done. The judgment stays with me.
