Your Phone Isn't Broken. It's Full of Photos.

September 30, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

Your phone says "Storage Almost Full."

It's not broken. It's just full. And it's almost always photos.

Here's the thing nobody tells you. You don't have to delete them. Deleting is the scary part, so people freeze and do nothing.

You can move them instead. Plug the phone into a computer with the cable that came in the box. The photos copy over. Then you can clear them off the phone.

If that sounds like too much, do it in small bites. One month at a time. Start with the oldest.

Ask me and I'll walk you through it, one step at a time — no rush, no jargon.

Two questions:

  1. What does your phone say when you try to take a new picture?
  2. Do you know where your photos actually live right now?
