Somebody's deciding whether to drive over.

They pull up your listing. There's a picture of your shop.

Shutter down. Lot empty. Flat grey light. Maybe some leaves on the sidewalk.

It's a real picture. Nobody faked it. It's just from a morning you were closed.

The camera car drives by when it wants. A holiday. An early Sunday. The week you were on the mainland. It doesn't call ahead and it doesn't come back.

So that's the picture doing the talking. Not your sign. Not your hours.

A shop that looks shut gets skipped. Quietly. Every day.

**Look at the picture on your listing. What does a stranger decide from it?**

Two questions.

1. Does that picture show your shop the way it looks when you're open?
2. If it doesn't, whose job is it to fix?
