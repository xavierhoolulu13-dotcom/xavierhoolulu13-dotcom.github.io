# You Don't Have a Website. You Have a Facebook Page.

Somebody hears about you. Types your name into their phone.

Up comes a Facebook page. Maybe it's yours. Maybe it's one somebody made for you years ago.

They tap it. And Facebook asks them to log in first.

They don't have an account. Or they do, and they can't remember the password. Either way, they hit a wall.

So they back out. And they call the next place.

A Facebook page isn't a website. It's a room inside somebody else's building. You don't own the door. You can't fix what's behind it.

**Type your business name into your phone. See what a stranger sees. Then tell me what comes up.**

Two questions:

1. If you searched your own business right now, what would a stranger find?
2. Do you have a page you actually own?

— Xavier
