Stop Typing. Tap the Little Microphone.

October 24, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

My aunt types with one finger. It took her four minutes to write "see you at five."

The keyboard is the smallest thing on the phone. Twelve letters under a thumb, and every one of them the wrong one. Then autocorrect turns it into a word she never said, and she sends it anyway, because starting over is worse.

Here's the part nobody shows you. There's a microphone on your keyboard. Tap it and talk.

Say the message out loud, at normal speed. Tap the microphone again when you're done. The words appear.

You still read it before you send it. That part doesn't change. Fix a word if it came out wrong, then send.

Find the little microphone on your keyboard. Tap it and say hello to somebody.

Two questions.

  1. Who would you text more often, if it didn't take four minutes?
  2. Has autocorrect ever sent something you never said?
