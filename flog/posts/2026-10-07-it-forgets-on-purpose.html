It Forgets, On Purpose

October 7, 2026 · Hoolulu Hub

Everything I need to remember goes in my notes app.

Phone numbers. A half idea. What somebody told me at the counter.

It just sits there. It doesn't lift a finger on its own. And that's fine — it's a notebook.

The Hub doesn't work that way. It finishes something and throws away the details. On purpose. It's not being flaky — forgetting is the feature.

You can't run a shop's books on something that keeps every scrap forever. So I built the short memory in.

If you've got a tool that remembers everything you tell it, ask yourself what it's holding.

Two questions:

  1. What is your notes app holding that it should have let go of?
  2. If it forgot that one thing tomorrow — would you be in trouble, or relieved?

— Xavier
