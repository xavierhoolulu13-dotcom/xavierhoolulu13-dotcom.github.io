The Back Office Fits in Your Pocket Now

September 29, 2026 · Hoolulu Hub

Nobody opens a shop because they love paperwork.

You open it to make the thing. Poke. Cuts. Coffee. Whatever's on your sign.

Then the day fills up with the other stuff. The invoice. The reminder. The follow-up. The post you meant to write. Small jobs, none of them hard, all of them yours.

That's what Hoolulu Hub is for. It's a workspace where the back-office work runs itself. You set the job once. It keeps going.

Mine runs on a phone. That's the whole point — I don't sit at a desk. I'm on Kalākaua with a plate lunch in one hand.

Tell me the one chore you'd hand off first if you could.

Two questions:

  1. What's the job you keep meaning to do and never get to?
  2. If it just... happened every week without you, what would you do with that hour?

— Xavier
