Somebody clicks "Book Now" on your site. Nothing happens.

Not an error page. Nothing. The button just sits there.

Here's why. That button points at a page. Somebody renamed the page, or deleted it, or moved the menu. The button still points at the old spot. It's been pointing at nothing for a year.

You'd never know. You don't click your own buttons. You already know where everything is.

**Open your site. Click every link on it. Every one. Write down the ones that go nowhere.**

Two questions.

1. When did you last click your own buttons?
2. What's the one page on your site you'd hate to lose?
