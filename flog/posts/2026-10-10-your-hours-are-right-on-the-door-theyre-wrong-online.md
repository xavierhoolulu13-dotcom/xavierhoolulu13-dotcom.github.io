The hand-written hours are taped to your door. You update them when they change. Holidays, early close, whatever.

Somebody checks before they drive over. Not the door. The phone.

And the hours they find are last year's. Closed at six. You're open till eight.

So they don't come. You never know why.

A wrong number of hours doesn't look like a mistake. It looks like you're closed.

**Open your own listing on your phone. Read your hours out loud. Do they match the sign on your door?**

Two questions:

1. Do the hours on your listing match the sign on your door right now?

2. When did you last change your hours — and did the listing get changed too?
