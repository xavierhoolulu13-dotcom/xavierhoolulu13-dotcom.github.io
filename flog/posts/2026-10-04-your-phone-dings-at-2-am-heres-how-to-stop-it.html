Your Phone Dings at 2 AM. Here's How to Stop It.

October 4, 2026 · Senior online help and how-to

Your phone goes off at 2 in the morning.

A ding. A buzz on the nightstand. You're awake now.

It's almost never anything. A store you bought from once. A news alert. Somebody's group text.

Here's how to stop it. Open Settings. Find Notifications. You'll see a list of every app on the phone. Go down the list and turn off the ones you don't need waking you up.

Keep the important ones on. Calls from family. Your doctor. Turn the rest off.

You can do this. It's just a list, and you're allowed to say no to it.

  2. How many apps are allowed to ring at 2 AM?
