Nobody Takes a Two-Hour-a-Week Job

October 3, 2026 · Hoolulu Hub

There's a job in your shop that takes about two hours a week.

You can't hire for it. Nobody wants two hours a week. And by the time you'd explain it, train them, check the work — you've spent the two hours anyway.

So it's yours. You do it at 11 at night. Or you don't, and it sits there getting older.

That's the job Hoolulu Hub is for. The one too small to hire for.

You don't hire a person. You set up a worker. It takes the two hours. You get the evening back.

Tell me the two-hour job you've been doing yourself at 11 PM.

Two questions:

  1. What's the job that's too small to hire for and too big to ignore?
  2. How long has it been sitting on your list?
