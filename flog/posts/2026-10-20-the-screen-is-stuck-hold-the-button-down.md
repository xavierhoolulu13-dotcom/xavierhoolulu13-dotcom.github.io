The screen freezes. Maybe on a photo. Maybe on a text you were halfway through. Maybe on nothing at all — just a white page.

You tap it. You tap harder. You hold it up and look at it, like that'll help.

It doesn't move.

Here's the part nobody tells you. That button on the side — the one you use to wake it up — it does more than wake it.

Hold it. Don't tap it. Hold it down.

If a slider shows up, slide it across. If nothing shows up, keep holding. Ten seconds. Maybe more. The screen goes dark. Then hold that same button and the logo comes back.

Nothing gets erased. Your pictures were never in the screen. The screen is just the window.

**Try it tonight, before you need it. Hold the button. Watch it come back.**

2. Did anybody ever show you that button — or did you find it yourself?
