You're Not on the List

September 24, 2026 · Honolulu local business visibility

Watch somebody look for lunch.

Phone out. Two words. "Poke near me." Three names come up. Maybe four. That's the whole list.

You're not on it.

Nothing wrong with your food. Nothing wrong with your door. You just never claimed the little map card with your name on it.

Hours nobody updated. No photos. Reviews sitting there with no reply. Google reads all of that, then decides who to show.

That's most of it. Being findable is a chore, not a mystery.

Look yourself up the way a stranger would. Not by name - just "what you do, near me."

Two questions:

  1. When you do that, are you in the first three?
  2. Who's sitting in that spot instead?

— Xavier
