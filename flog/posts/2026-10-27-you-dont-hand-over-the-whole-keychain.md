When you hire somebody, you give them a key.

Not the safe combination. Not the bank login. One key, one door, the hours they work.

Automation should work the same way. Most of it doesn't.

Most tools want everything. Your email. Your calendar. Your whole account. All of it or none of it. So people either hand over the master key or never start at all.

Hoolulu Hub lets you hand over one drawer.

The calendar. The inbox. The invoice folder. Whatever the job actually needs. Not the rest.

If it only answers the phone, it doesn't get the checkbook.

**Start with one small key. See how it does. Then hand over the next one.**

Two questions.

1. What's the one thing you'd never hand over?
2. Who in your shop is holding the master key right now?
